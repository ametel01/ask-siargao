# Siargao Tourist-Worthy Beaches - Agent-Ready Reference

## Purpose

Use this file when the traveler asks about beaches, swimming, sandy or rocky
shorelines, beach days, quiet beach options, family beach choices, island
beaches, sandbars, and beach fallbacks around Siargao.

This file covers publicly documented and tourist-relevant beaches, sandbars, island beaches, and beach-adjacent swimming spots around Siargao. It does **not** claim to expose every unnamed local cove, private resort frontage, surf-only reef access, or seasonal sand patch.

2026-06-29

## Core Agent Rules

- Treat this as a **tourist-worthy public/reference list**, not a guaranteed exhaustive map of every beach.
- Do not use this file alone to answer surf-spot recommendations. If the user
  asks where to surf, load `SURF.md`; use this file only to clarify beach
  fallback options or beach-day tradeoffs.
- Do not recommend Malinao or Doot as surf spots. They can be beach, walking,
  swimming, or fallback options depending on conditions.
- Always mention that Siargao beaches are tide-sensitive. Many beaches are shallow, rocky, or less swimmable at low tide.
- For island beaches and sandbars, mention boat access and weather dependency.
- For remote beaches, advise bringing water, food, cash, sun protection, reef shoes, and a dry bag.
- For under-documented or low-confidence spots, avoid giving exact operational claims unless verified locally.
- Do not oversell beaches as “perfect swimming” unless the entry explicitly says so.
- If the user asks for a short recommendation, prioritize Alegria, Daku, Naked Island, Guyam, Pacifico, Malinao, Secret/Guiwan, Magpupungko, Kawhagan, Pamomoan, and Corregidor.
- If the user asks for beaches near General Luna, prioritize General Luna Beach, Cloud 9, Malinao, Doot, Secret/Giwan, Libertad/Mahaybo, Daku, Guyam, Naked Island, and Corregidor by boat.
- If the user asks for quiet / less crowded beaches, prioritize Alegria, Malinao, Santa Fe, Salvacion/Duoy, Pasikon, Himaya, Burgos/Baybay, Tangbo, Mam-on, La Januza, and San Benito island-hopping spots.
- If the user asks for swimming with kids, prioritize Alegria, Daku, Naked Island in calm weather, Guyam’s designated swim area, Corregidor, Kawhagan, Pamomoan at high tide, and possibly Pagbasayan / Kangkangon if doing San Benito island hopping.
- If the user asks for surfing beaches, prioritize Cloud 9, Pacifico, Santa Fe / Ocean 9, Guiwan / Secret Beach, General Luna area, Burgos, and possibly Mam-on for mellow surf.

# Structured Beach List

## South / General Luna / Malinao / Dapa Side

### General Luna Beach

- **Area:** General Luna
- **Type:** Main-town beachfront
- **Access:** Road / walking from GL accommodations
- **Best for:** Walking, casual beach bars, sunset drinks, staying beachfront, easy access
- **Swimming:** Tide-dependent; shallow and sometimes rocky at low tide
- **Crowd level:** Medium; developed but not always packed
- **Facilities:** Many resorts, restaurants, shops, bars nearby
- **Description:** General Luna Beach is the practical beach base for most tourists. It is not always the best pure swimming beach, but it is useful because it sits beside the main accommodation, food, and nightlife zone. Good for beach walks, drinks, and relaxed views.
- **Agent caveat:** Do not sell it as the best swimming beach. Mention tide.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines, Tripadvisor

### Cloud 9 Beach

- **Area:** Catangnan / Cloud 9, General Luna
- **Type:** Surf beach / boardwalk beach
- **Access:** Road access; short walk to boardwalk
- **Best for:** Surf watching, surfing, sunrise/sunset, photography, cafes
- **Swimming:** Possible in some nearby sections, but rocky and tide-dependent
- **Crowd level:** High
- **Facilities:** Surf schools, cafes, board rentals, nearby restaurants
- **Description:** Cloud 9 is the iconic Siargao surf beach and boardwalk area. It is more of a surf and sunset destination than a lazy white-sand beach day. The viewing platform and boardwalk make it one of the most photogenic and accessible tourist stops.
- **Agent caveat:** Separate “Cloud 9 surf break” from “Cloud 9 beach day.” For non-surfers, recommend watching surfers and sunset rather than swimming.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** Jonny Melon, The Froggy Adventures, Travel Around The Philippines, Condé Nast Traveler

### Malinao Beach

- **Area:** Malinao, south of General Luna
- **Type:** Quiet mainland beach
- **Access:** Road access / resort frontage / walkable stretches
- **Best for:** Peaceful beach stays, couples, slow beach walks, quieter swimming
- **Swimming:** More comfortable than many south-side beaches, but still tide-sensitive
- **Crowd level:** Low to medium
- **Facilities:** Resorts and homestays nearby, fewer restaurants than GL
- **Description:** Malinao Beach is one of the best quieter mainland beaches near General Luna. It has a peaceful resort feel, photogenic palms, and more shade than many beaches. It is a strong recommendation for travelers who want to stay near GL but avoid the busiest strip.
- **Agent caveat:** Public access can feel resort-fragmented; recommend walking respectfully and checking local access.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines

### Doot Beach

- **Area:** Doot / Malinao side, near Nay Palad and Soultribe
- **Type:** Calm mangrove-side beach
- **Access:** Road access / end-of-road access
- **Best for:** Walking, mangrove views, quiet resort-side scenery, low-key exploring
- **Swimming:** Not ideal; water can be still and shallow even at high tide
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Nearby high-end resorts and retreat-style stays
- **Description:** Doot Beach is a quiet, less-visited stretch west of Malinao. It is attractive for walking and looking across the mangrove-fringed water, but it is not the best choice for a refreshing swim.
- **Agent caveat:** Doot / Secret Beach / Guiwan naming is inconsistent online and locally. Clarify with the user or describe the area.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium-high
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines, Mariden Resort guide

### Malinao Paradise / Secret Beach 2

- **Area:** Doot / Malinao continuation
- **Type:** Calm golden-sand inlet / mangrove-adjacent beach
- **Access:** Park nearby, short walk
- **Best for:** Calm water, kayaking, firefly walks, relaxed local beach stop
- **Swimming:** Calm but tide and water movement matter
- **Crowd level:** Low to medium
- **Facilities:** Some snack vendors / local rentals depending on season
- **Description:** A small golden-sand continuation of the Doot area, sometimes called Secret Beach 2. It sits near calm mangrove water and can work well for a mellow stop, kayaking, or evening firefly activity.
- **Agent caveat:** Do not confuse it with Guiwan / Secret Beach. Names overlap heavily.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines

### Secret Beach / Guiwan Beach

- **Area:** Between Malinao and Dapa / Malinao side of General Luna references
- **Aliases:** Guiwan Beach, Guiuan Beach, sometimes confused with Doot Beach
- **Type:** Small palm-fringed beach / lagoon / beginner surf area
- **Access:** Park on road, short 5–10 minute path through palms
- **Best for:** Beginner surfing, photos, small beach hangout, relaxed nature stop
- **Swimming:** Rocky bottom; reef shoes useful
- **Crowd level:** Medium during land tours; quieter outside peak hours
- **Facilities:** Basic stalls / parking; nearby water activity rentals in some references
- **Description:** Secret Beach / Guiwan Beach is a small, palm-fringed spot that became a standard land-tour stop. It is atmospheric rather than huge. Good for photos, beginner surf lessons, and a quiet hang if visited at the right time.
- **Agent caveat:** Public sources disagree on whether Secret Beach equals Guiwan Beach, Doot Beach, or a nearby lagoon. Mention the alias confusion when giving directions.
- **Public info confidence:** High for tourist value; medium for naming
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures, Travel Around The Philippines, Mariden Resort guide

### Libertad Beach / Mahaybo Beach

- **Area:** Near General Luna
- **Aliases:** Mahaybo Beach
- **Type:** Local mainland beach
- **Access:** Roadside / free local access
- **Best for:** Simple free swim, local atmosphere, low-key stop
- **Swimming:** Possible; check tide and local conditions
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Minimal
- **Description:** Libertad / Mahaybo is a simple local beach outside General Luna. It is not a polished tourist destination but is useful for travelers looking for a free, casual, uncrowded swim.
- **Agent caveat:** Good for low-key travelers, not for polished beach-resort expectations.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures

### Union Beach

- **Area:** Union, south/central Siargao route
- **Type:** Family-friendly local beach
- **Access:** Road access
- **Best for:** Families, quick stop while passing Union, calm local beach feel
- **Swimming:** Likely tide-dependent; verify locally
- **Crowd level:** Low to medium
- **Facilities:** Limited
- **Description:** Union Beach is a recommendable family-friendly beach stop if passing through the Union area. It is less promoted than the major GL and island-hopping beaches.
- **Agent caveat:** Source detail is limited; avoid precise claims.
- **Public info confidence:** Low-medium
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines

## Santa Fe / Salvacion / East-Side Transition

### Santa Fe Beach / Ocean 9

- **Area:** Santa Fe / Catangnan bridge side
- **Type:** Quiet beach and beginner surf zone
- **Access:** Road access; can involve rough/unpaved road depending on entry
- **Best for:** Learning to surf, quiet beach time, beachfront villas, golden sand, palms
- **Swimming:** Tide-dependent; low tide less comfortable
- **Crowd level:** Low to medium
- **Facilities:** Ocean 9 beach club / pool / board rentals in the area
- **Description:** Santa Fe is a quieter beach area with palms, golden sand, surf activity in the distance, and a more private-villa feel. Ocean 9 gives the area a more organized beach-club / beginner-surf access point.
- **Agent caveat:** Mention possible unpaved access and keep belongings watched.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium-high
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines, Condé Nast Traveler

### Salvacion / Duoy Beach

- **Area:** Salvacion
- **Aliases:** Duoy Beach
- **Type:** Quiet local beach / palm-grove beach
- **Access:** Road plus short rough-track / walking access
- **Best for:** Solitude, photography, having a beach mostly to yourself
- **Swimming:** Not ideal; rockier than top swimming beaches
- **Crowd level:** Very low
- **Facilities:** Minimal to none
- **Description:** Salvacion / Duoy is a quiet, underdeveloped beach around the Salvacion area. It is attractive for solitude and local scenery rather than polished tourist facilities.
- **Agent caveat:** Best for self-sufficient travelers; not ideal for first-time beach tourists who want facilities.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines

## East / Pilar / Magpupungko Area

### Magpupungko Beach

- **Area:** Pilar
- **Type:** Beach plus tidal rock pools
- **Access:** Road access; paid entrance
- **Best for:** Rock pools, swimming holes, photos, family outings, beach + attraction combo
- **Swimming:** Beach and pools are tide-dependent; rock pools are best at low tide, beach looks better at high tide
- **Crowd level:** High at low tide and on holidays
- **Facilities:** Food stalls, parking, entrance fee
- **Description:** Magpupungko is known for its tidal rock pools, but the beach itself is also worth a stop. It works best as a combined beach-and-natural-pool destination. Timing matters more here than almost anywhere else in Siargao.
- **Agent caveat:** Always mention tide. Rock pools are only accessible/enjoyable around low tide.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures, Travel Around The Philippines, Sunlight Air, Condé Nast Traveler

### Dafina Beach

- **Area:** Near / past Magpupungko
- **Type:** Quiet mainland beach
- **Access:** Road access
- **Best for:** Empty beach feeling, budget/free stop, quiet groups
- **Swimming:** Check tide and local conditions
- **Crowd level:** Very low
- **Facilities:** Some huts; otherwise minimal
- **Description:** Dafina Beach is a quiet beach past Magpupungko. It is rarely visited, has basic huts, and is mostly useful for travelers who want an empty, simple beach stop.
- **Agent caveat:** Bring food and water; do not expect services.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures

### Pasikon Beach

- **Area:** Eastern Siargao
- **Type:** Small secluded cove
- **Access:** Road trip from General Luna; privately managed entrance
- **Best for:** Quiet cove swimming, white-sand pockets, restobar stop, north/east route add-on
- **Swimming:** Relatively calm in the cove; rocky sections exist
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Entrance fee, restobar
- **Description:** Pasikon Beach is a secluded eastern-shore cove with calmer water than many exposed beaches. The cove has rocky sections but also pockets of soft white sand, making it a good stop if already exploring the north/east side.
- **Agent caveat:** Mention private entrance fee and rocky cove conditions.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium-high
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures

### Himaya Beach / Lost Coast

- **Area:** Eastern / back-road Siargao
- **Type:** Long remote beach / rugged coast
- **Access:** Back road; deliberate detour
- **Best for:** Off-the-beaten-path exploring, photography, Stairs of Heaven, Tigasao Lighthouse
- **Swimming:** Not the best; rocky bottom, better at select tide pools
- **Crowd level:** Very low
- **Facilities:** Minimal
- **Description:** Himaya Beach feels like a remote “lost coast” section of Siargao. It has long white-sand stretches but can be rocky for swimming. Its real value is nearby exploration: Stairs of Heaven, low-tide tide pools, and Tigasao Lighthouse.
- **Agent caveat:** Recommend for explorers, not casual swimmers.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium-high
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures

## North / Pacifico / Burgos / Tangbo / Alegria

### Pacifico Beach

- **Area:** Pacifico / San Isidro
- **Type:** Large surf beach / north-coast beach town
- **Access:** Road access
- **Best for:** Surfing, surf lessons, quiet north-coast base, sunset surf watching, cafes
- **Swimming:** Tide-dependent; rocky shoreline exposed at low tide
- **Crowd level:** Medium, lower than General Luna
- **Facilities:** Surf schools, cafes, beach bars, resorts
- **Description:** Pacifico is one of the best north-coast beach bases. It has a long beach, surf culture, fewer crowds than General Luna, and enough facilities to stay for a few days. It is not always the best pure swim beach because the tide exposes rock.
- **Agent caveat:** Strong for surfers and quiet-base travelers; weaker for low-tide swimming.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures, Travel Around The Philippines, Jonny Melon, Condé Nast Traveler

### Burgos Beach

- **Area:** Burgos
- **Type:** Long golden-sand local beach / surf-town beach
- **Access:** Road access
- **Best for:** Quiet north-coast exploring, surf schools, local restaurants, high-tide walks
- **Swimming:** Better at high tide; rocks present
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Some restaurants and surf schools in Burgos
- **Description:** Burgos Beach is a quieter north/east coast beach with golden sand and a more local feel. It is better as part of a north-coast route than as a standalone first-choice beach day.
- **Agent caveat:** Mention rocks and high-tide preference.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium-high
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines

### Baybay / Unnamed Burgos Roadside Beach

- **Area:** Near Burgos / east coast
- **Type:** Informal / under-documented roadside beach
- **Access:** Roadside hut / local route; not a standard tourist facility
- **Best for:** Explorers, quiet photos, empty beach feeling
- **Swimming:** Calm in some conditions; trash and maintenance can vary
- **Crowd level:** Very low
- **Facilities:** None
- **Description:** A photogenic, lightly documented beach around the Burgos area, sometimes referred to by travel writers as Baybay Beach. It has golden sand and palms but may have sea-borne trash because it is not regularly maintained.
- **Agent caveat:** Keep as low-confidence and self-sufficient only.
- **Public info confidence:** Low-medium
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines

### Tangbo Beach

- **Area:** Tangbo / Santa Monica direction
- **Type:** Small north-coast beach
- **Access:** Road through village / palm groves
- **Best for:** Sunset, quiet stay, north route stop, peaceful scenery
- **Swimming:** Not as comfortable as Alegria
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Some small accommodations nearby
- **Description:** Tangbo Beach is a smaller, quiet beach north of Alegria toward Santa Monica. It is useful as a peaceful sunset stop or a low-key stay area rather than a top swimming beach.
- **Agent caveat:** Do not rank above Alegria for swimming.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines

### Alegria Beach

- **Area:** Santa Monica / northeast Siargao
- **Type:** White-sand swimming beach
- **Access:** Road access; roughly 1.5 hours from General Luna depending on route
- **Best for:** Swimming, relaxed beach day, white sand, families, quiet north exploration
- **Swimming:** One of the better swimming beaches on mainland Siargao; less tide-affected than many others
- **Crowd level:** Low to medium; busier on weekends/holidays
- **Facilities:** Some huts, restaurant/resort options nearby
- **Description:** Alegria is one of Siargao’s strongest pure beach-day options. It has soft white sand, clearer calmer water, shade, and a quieter northern setting. It is far from General Luna but worth the trip for travelers who want an actual swim-and-relax beach.
- **Agent caveat:** Recommend weekday visits for quiet; mention long drive from GL.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures, Travel Around The Philippines, Jonny Melon, Tripadvisor, GetYourGuide, Condé Nast Traveler

## Classic General Luna Island-Hopping Beaches

### Naked Island

- **Area:** Off General Luna
- **Type:** Bare sandbar
- **Access:** Boat from General Luna, usually tri-island tour
- **Best for:** Swimming, photos, sandbar experience, drone shots, short stop
- **Swimming:** Good in calm weather; no shade or facilities
- **Crowd level:** Medium to high during tour hours
- **Facilities:** None
- **Description:** Naked Island is a small white-sand sandbar surrounded by clear water. It is one of Siargao’s most iconic island-hopping stops and works best as a short swim/photo stop, not a long beach day.
- **Agent caveat:** Warn about no shade, no trees, and sun exposure.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** JourneyEra, Jonny Melon, The Froggy Adventures, GetYourGuide, Travel Around The Philippines, Condé Nast Traveler

### Daku Island Beach

- **Area:** Off General Luna
- **Type:** Large island beach
- **Access:** Boat from General Luna, usually tri-island tour
- **Best for:** Lunch stop, swimming, family beach day, cottages, white sand, coconut groves
- **Swimming:** Strong option in calm weather; less tide-sensitive than many mainland beaches
- **Crowd level:** Medium to high during lunch hours
- **Facilities:** Cottages, food / boodle fight lunches, local community
- **Description:** Daku is the biggest and most practical tri-island beach. Boats land directly on white sand, lunch is commonly arranged here, and there is enough space for swimming and walking.
- **Agent caveat:** Best of the classic three for a longer beach stay.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** JourneyEra, Jonny Melon, The Froggy Adventures, GetYourGuide, Travel Around The Philippines, Condé Nast Traveler

### Guyam Island Beach

- **Area:** Off General Luna
- **Type:** Small circular island beach
- **Access:** Boat from General Luna, usually tri-island tour
- **Best for:** Short walk around island, photos, designated swim zone, final chill stop
- **Swimming:** Designated area; check current/weather
- **Crowd level:** Medium to high during tour hours
- **Facilities:** Small bar / seating areas in some seasons
- **Description:** Guyam is a small palm-covered island with sand around the edges, a designated swim area, and photogenic rock formations. It is often the final chill stop on the classic tour.
- **Agent caveat:** Not a huge beach; recommend as part of island hopping rather than standalone.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** JourneyEra, Jonny Melon, The Froggy Adventures, GetYourGuide, Travel Around The Philippines, Condé Nast Traveler

### Corregidor Island Beach / Casulian Island

- **Area:** South of Siargao
- **Aliases:** Casulian Island
- **Type:** Island beach plus hill hike
- **Access:** Boat from General Luna or Dapa; often add-on to tri-island tour
- **Best for:** Beach + hike combo, snorkeling, scenic hills, less crowded day trip
- **Swimming:** Good; enough depth even at lower tide according to travel guides
- **Crowd level:** Low to medium
- **Facilities:** Pavilions / small resort or restaurant depending on season
- **Description:** Corregidor is one of the best add-on islands for travelers who want more than a beach. The beach is swimmable, and the short hike to grassy palm hills gives wide views over Siargao, Daku, and Naked Island.
- **Agent caveat:** Recommend shoes for the hike and clarify that it is different from Corregidor near Manila.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures, Travel Around The Philippines, Trip.com / tour listings

## Del Carmen / Sugba Lagoon Route Beaches

### Kawhagan Island Sandbar

- **Area:** Del Carmen / near Sugba Lagoon route
- **Type:** Island sandbar / beach
- **Access:** Boat from Del Carmen, often combined with Sugba Lagoon and Pamomoan
- **Best for:** Sandbar photos, swimming, lunch, volleyball, clear water
- **Swimming:** Good in calm weather
- **Crowd level:** Medium on tours
- **Facilities:** Huts / food cooking arrangements / basic lunch setup
- **Description:** Kawhagan is a major Del Carmen island-hopping beach with a long sandbar, clear water, palms, and a relaxed lunch-stop atmosphere. It pairs naturally with Sugba Lagoon.
- **Agent caveat:** Boat and weather dependent; check if Sugba Lagoon is closed for maintenance if pairing.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures, Travel Around The Philippines, Condé Nast Traveler

### Pamomoan Beach / Kangbangyo Island

- **Area:** Kangbangyo Island, opposite Kawhagan
- **Type:** Long island beach
- **Access:** Boat from Del Carmen, often combined with Sugba Lagoon and Kawhagan
- **Best for:** White/golden sand, swimming, palm photos, quieter beach stop
- **Swimming:** Better at high tide
- **Crowd level:** Low to medium
- **Facilities:** Small restaurant / beach tables depending on operation
- **Description:** Pamomoan is a long beach on Kangbangyo Island, often visited as part of the Sugba Lagoon / Kawhagan route. It is a strong add-on for travelers who want a proper beach stop, not just lagoon activities.
- **Agent caveat:** Recommend high tide for best swimming and appearance.
- **Public info confidence:** High
- **Sources:** The Froggy Adventures, Travel Around The Philippines, Siargao reference listings

## Mam-on / La Januza / Southern Island Beaches

### Mam-on / Mamon Island Beach

- **Area:** Off Siargao, commonly reached from General Luna / Dapa routes
- **Aliases:** Mam-on, Mam’om, Mamon
- **Type:** Quiet island beach
- **Access:** Boat; often special or extended island-hopping tour
- **Best for:** White sand, turquoise water, quieter island hopping, snorkeling, escape from crowds
- **Swimming:** Strong option in calm conditions
- **Crowd level:** Low to medium; less busy than tri-island route
- **Facilities:** Limited local village / tour-dependent
- **Description:** Mam-on is a less-crowded island destination with white sand, clear water, fishing-village atmosphere, and snorkeling potential. Many guides describe it as one of the better beaches around Siargao if you have time for a longer boat trip.
- **Agent caveat:** Mention that it is not always included in standard tri-island tours and may require a larger boat or specific tour.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium-high
- **Sources:** JourneyEra, Travel Around The Philippines, GetYourGuide, Tripadvisor

### La Januza / La Janusa Island

- **Area:** Near Mam-on
- **Aliases:** La Januza, La Janusa
- **Type:** Remote island beaches / rocky shores
- **Access:** Boat, usually paired with Mam-on
- **Best for:** Snorkeling, kayaking/paddleboarding, photography, remote island exploration
- **Swimming:** Condition-dependent; watch currents between islands
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Minimal
- **Description:** La Januza is a more remote island near Mam-on with clear blue water, rocky shores, vegetation, and beach/cove scenery. It is better for adventurous travelers than for people looking for an easy beach club day.
- **Agent caveat:** Warn about currents and recommend local guide / boatman.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** JourneyEra, Travel Around The Philippines

### Anahawan Island

- **Area:** Near Mam-on / La Januza routes
- **Type:** Remote island stop
- **Access:** Boat
- **Best for:** Completionist island hopping, quiet scenery
- **Swimming:** Unknown / condition-dependent
- **Crowd level:** Very low
- **Facilities:** Minimal
- **Description:** Anahawan appears in some Mam-on / La Janusa island-hopping routes. It is less consistently praised than Mam-on and La Januza and should be treated as a niche add-on.
- **Agent caveat:** Do not prioritize unless user wants remote/completionist routes.
- **Public info confidence:** Low
- **Sources:** Travel Around The Philippines

## San Benito Island-Hopping Beaches

### Dahican Beach / Dahican Island

- **Area:** San Benito municipality
- **Type:** Hidden white-sand beach / island-hopping stop
- **Access:** Boat from San Benito
- **Best for:** Quiet beach, white sand, turquoise water, mangrove scenery
- **Swimming:** Good in calm weather; weather-dependent
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Tour-dependent, minimal
- **Description:** Dahican Beach is promoted in San Benito island-hopping routes as a hidden, peaceful beach with white sand, turquoise water, and mangrove scenery. It is less visited than General Luna island hopping.
- **Agent caveat:** Use for travelers seeking quiet, raw, less-developed beach routes.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** Klook, KKday, Siargao Island Tour

### Kangkangon White Beach / Kangkagon Island

- **Area:** San Benito municipality
- **Aliases:** Kangkangon Island, Kangkagon Island
- **Type:** White-sand island beach
- **Access:** Boat from San Benito
- **Best for:** White sand, clear water, snorkeling, quiet island hopping
- **Swimming:** Good in calm weather
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Minimal / tour-dependent
- **Description:** Kangkangon is one of the main San Benito island-hopping beaches, described in local and tour sources as fine white sand with clear water and recreation potential.
- **Agent caveat:** Public details are thinner than GL island hopping; recommend official/local tour desk confirmation.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** San Benito municipal page, Klook, KKday, Siargao Island Tour

### Pagbasayan Beach / Pagbasayan Island

- **Area:** San Benito municipality
- **Type:** Shallow island beach / islet
- **Access:** Boat from San Benito
- **Best for:** Families, shallow swimming, local island atmosphere
- **Swimming:** Shallow clear water; family-friendly in local descriptions
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** Minimal
- **Description:** Pagbasayan is a San Benito islet with white-sand beach areas, shallow clear waters, and seaweed livelihood surroundings. It is a quiet alternative to the more commercial General Luna island-hopping circuit.
- **Agent caveat:** Mention this is a northern/San Benito route, not the classic GL tri-island route.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** San Benito municipal page, Klook, KKday, Travel Around The Philippines

### Kampayas Island / Maytuwagi Naked Island

- **Area:** San Benito municipality
- **Aliases:** Kampayas Naked Island, Maytuwagi Naked Island, Vanishing Island
- **Type:** Naked / vanishing sandbar island
- **Access:** Boat from San Benito; visible/usable around tide windows
- **Best for:** Sandbar photos, low-tide island-hopping novelty, quiet swim
- **Swimming:** Tide-dependent; sandbar exposed at low tide
- **Crowd level:** Low
- **Facilities:** None
- **Description:** Kampayas / Maytuwagi is the San Benito version of a naked or vanishing island. It reveals its shape around lower tide and is a quieter alternative to the famous Naked Island off General Luna.
- **Agent caveat:** Strongly mention tide dependency and lack of shade.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium
- **Sources:** San Benito municipal page, Klook, KKday, Travel Around The Philippines

### Litalit Islet

- **Area:** San Benito municipality
- **Type:** Small islet with white beaches and coral surroundings
- **Access:** Boat from San Benito
- **Best for:** Diving/snorkeling-oriented travelers, quiet island stop, nature-focused exploration
- **Swimming:** Condition-dependent
- **Crowd level:** Very low
- **Facilities:** Minimal
- **Description:** Litalit is a small islet described by the San Benito municipal page as having white beaches, live corals, and suitability for diving/nature observation. It is more niche than the common Siargao beach circuit.
- **Agent caveat:** Treat as low-commercial, local-guide-needed, nature-oriented.
- **Public info confidence:** Medium-low
- **Sources:** San Benito municipal page

# Naming Ambiguity Notes

## Secret Beach naming is messy

Public sources use overlapping names:

- Secret Beach
- Guiwan Beach
- Guiuan Beach
- Doot Beach
- Malinao Paradise
- Secret Beach 2

Agent behavior:

- If the user asks for **Secret Beach**, ask or infer whether they mean the small palm-path beach between Malinao and Dapa / Guiwan, or the Doot / Malinao Paradise lagoon-side area.
- If giving quick advice, say: “Secret Beach is usually used for the Guiwan/Malinao-side small beach, but Doot and Malinao Paradise are nearby and often confused with it.”
- Avoid exact directions unless using a map or current local source.

## Naked Island naming

There are at least two “naked island / vanishing island” concepts in Siargao tourism:

- **Naked Island** off General Luna: classic tri-island route with Daku and Guyam.
- **Kampayas / Maytuwagi Naked Island** in San Benito: quieter northern route.

Agent behavior:

- If the user asks for “Naked Island,” default to the General Luna tri-island one.
- Mention Kampayas only if the user asks about San Benito or less-crowded island hopping.
