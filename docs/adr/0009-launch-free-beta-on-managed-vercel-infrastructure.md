# Launch Free Controlled Beta on managed Vercel infrastructure

Ask Siargao will deploy Protected Staging and then the Free Controlled Beta on Vercel, backed by
managed PostgreSQL, managed Redis, and managed scheduled jobs co-located in Singapore rather than
self-hosting production state or preserving platform portability for this launch. This accepts
Vercel and provider coupling plus a single-region availability limit to fit one named Operator and a
USD 300 monthly fixed-infrastructure ceiling; live Stripe production resources remain deferred
until the independently authorized Checkout Canary, while the accepted recovery floor remains an
RPO of 15 minutes, an RTO of four hours, at least seven days of backup retention, and monthly restore
drills.
