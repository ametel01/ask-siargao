# Retain only minimized accounting evidence for the applicable record period

Ask Siargao retains account-detached accounting evidence until the later of five years from the
applicable Philippine tax-return deadline or actual filing date, or resolution of a relevant audit,
refund, dispute, or claim. This working period requires professional confirmation of the owner's
actual tax jurisdiction before launch. Email, Account linkage, checkout URLs, raw provider payloads,
and per-answer detail keep their shorter purge deadlines; no record receives indefinite retention.
