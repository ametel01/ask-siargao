# Keep Travel Answers continuously available

Ask Siargao will accept new Travel Answers at all hours in Protected Staging and production instead
of coupling product availability to human staffing. The emergency exposure stop, shared daily
traffic limits, provider cost circuits, health checks, alerting, and rollback controls remain
enforced; this accepts best-effort incident response outside normal working hours without claiming
continuous human support. This decision supersedes ADR-0011.
