# Correlate visibility with privacy-safe journeys

Ask Siargao will measure qualified discovery through a short-lived Visibility Journey propagated
from public content through chat acceptance and answer processing, while counting conversion only
after Travel Answer admission succeeds. The journey carries bounded source dimensions but excludes
prompts, full referrer URLs, search queries, location, account identifiers, and other traveler
content; this accepts less granular attribution to avoid turning product analytics into an account
or behavior profile.
